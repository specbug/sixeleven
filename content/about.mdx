Hi, I'm Rishit. I'm a software engineer at [Abnormal AI](https://abnormal.ai/), where I work on scaling and running the data ingestion platform. Before that I was a founding engineer at a YC startup, where I built the core ML pipeline for retail demand forecasting and inventory optimisation.

I have a particular fondness for _starting_ things, though I'm still mastering the art of follow-through. This interest has sparked work across a wide range of fields: [an autograd engine](https://github.com/specbug/punygrad) in pure Python, learning the keyboard, [searching for an exoplanet](/blog/tic402710280), and [a system for learning anything](https://github.com/specbug/odyssey). I read sci-fi, rational fiction and grimdark fantasy, and unwind with a film, an RPG or a Souls-like. I've spent over 600 hours in Elden Ring.

_sixeleven_ is a nod to Gödel's incompleteness theorems, numbered VI and XI. It also describes the epistemic status of most things written here.
